Piano-Master feasibility tests 1, 2, 3 and 6 · no keyboard needed

1. Saved data after a relaunch

Tap "Save test values", close MIDIWeb Browser completely, reopen it and come back to this page. Local storage, the cookie and IndexedDB should say FOUND. Session storage is expected to be gone.

2. Keeping the screen awake

Set Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock to 2 minutes. Start one method, then don't touch the iPad for 4 minutes. If the screen locks, the log shows when the page was hidden. Test each method on its own.

3. Audio

Start audio, then try the metronome with the silent switch (or Control Center silent mode) on and off, and across a lock and wake.

Stems. Each test stem is a click every second over a tone that changes pitch every 10 seconds, so a jump is easy to hear. At the 50% preset a 3-minute song needs two 6-minute stems held in memory.

6. Spoken text and video

The video should play inside the page, not jump to a full-screen player, with sound.
